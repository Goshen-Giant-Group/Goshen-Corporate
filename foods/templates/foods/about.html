About Naturis Marketplace

Naturis Marketplace is the wholesale platform for Naturis Foods, a brand of Goshen Giant Foods Ltd., a subsidiary of Goshen Giant Group Ltd..

Goshen Giant Foods Ltd. is a Nigerian food and beverage company that sources, processes, packages and distributes West African foods for retail stores, restaurants and distributors. The company is committed to food safety, quality, sustainability and innovation, guided by applicable Nigerian and export-market requirements.

Products for wholesale buyers

Our catalogue includes whole and ground egusi, crayfish, smoked African catfish, white and yellow garri, traditional flours and red palm oil. Buyers select full, retail-ready master cartons and may combine products in a mixed-carton order.

How we supply your business

We arrange consolidated air cargo or ocean freight and deliver your cartons to the store or warehouse address named in your written quotation. The quotation confirms product availability, carton configuration, shipping method, applicable delivery terms and the total delivered cost before an order is accepted.

Company enquiries

Contact our team in Uyo, Akwa Ibom State, Nigeria, for product information and delivered wholesale quotations.
