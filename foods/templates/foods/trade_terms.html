Wholesale information

Trade terms

These are our standard quotation guidelines. Your written quotation and accepted order will specify the terms for your particular shipment.

Who may order

We supply retail stores, restaurants and distributors in full master cartons. Buyers may combine products across the catalogue.

Retail stores and restaurants: Minimum order: 10 mixed master cartons totaling at least 200 kg (approximately 441 lb) net. Typical orders contain 10–15 cartons and weigh approximately 200–300 kg (441–661 lb), depending on the product mix.

Large distributors: One full pallet and at least 500 kg net product weight. The final pallet build depends on actual carton measurements.

Indicative inclusive USD prices

Indicative USD prices for U.S. delivery include freight, customs clearance, duties, taxes and local delivery to your store or warehouse. The distributor tier is 10% below the store tier for qualifying orders. The written quotation confirms eligibility and the final inclusive price for your named address and order mix. No online payment is collected.

Smaller trial orders may be considered on request, subject to written approval. A trial enquiry does not change the standard order thresholds.

Shipping and delivery

Consolidated air cargo is the starting quotation for smaller store orders and ocean freight for distributor pallets. The alternative may be quoted where suitable. The written quotation confirms the shipping method, named delivery address, agreed delivery terms, estimated timing, and responsibility for customs clearance and import charges. Delivery timing depends on product availability and the agreed shipping route.

Availability, payment and quotation validity

A request through this website is an enquiry, not an accepted order. We confirm stock availability, quotation validity, payment method and payment schedule in the written quotation or invoice before the order is placed.

Shortage, damage and returns

Check cartons on delivery. Contact us promptly with your quotation or order reference, photographs of the affected cartons and product labels, and a description of any shortage or damage. We assess the issue against the accepted order terms and applicable requirements. Return eligibility and instructions are confirmed in the written quotation or agreed order terms; contact us before sending goods back.
