Frequently asked questions

Answers to common wholesale ordering and delivery questions.

Can retail stores mix different products in one order?

Yes. You can combine full master cartons from any products in the catalogue. A retail store or restaurant order must contain at least 10 cartons totaling at least 200 kg (approximately 441 lb) net. Typical orders contain 10–15 cartons and weigh approximately 200–300 kg (441–661 lb), depending on the product mix.

What is the minimum for a distributor order?

One full pallet and at least 500 kg (approximately 1,102 lb) net product weight. The pallet may contain mixed full master cartons; we confirm the pallet build in your quotation.

Do you sell individual packs?

Our website is for wholesale buyers. We supply complete master cartons, with the number of retail-ready packs shown on each product page.

What is included in the displayed prices?

Displayed USD prices are U.S. delivery references inclusive of freight, customs clearance, duties, taxes and local delivery. For other countries or currencies, request a destination-specific written quotation.

Can you deliver directly to my store or warehouse?

Yes. Provide your store or warehouse delivery address when requesting a quote. We arrange the agreed shipping method and delivery to the address named in your quotation.

Is shipping by air or ocean?

Consolidated air cargo is the starting option for a small-store order, and ocean freight is the starting option for a distributor pallet. We can quote the alternative where suitable for the order and destination.

How does the quotation request work?

Add full cartons, provide your business details and select “Prepare email request.” Your submitted request is saved and given a reference number for follow-up. We then prepare a written quotation. No payment or order is taken by this website.

Where can I find product storage and allergen details?

Select “Quick details” on any product card. Its specification view includes ingredients, packaging, storage, allergen information and product-specific questions.
