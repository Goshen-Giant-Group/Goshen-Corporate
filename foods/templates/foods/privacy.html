Legal & privacy

Privacy Notice

This privacy notice explains how Naturis Foods / Goshen Giant Foods Ltd. collects, uses, and protects the business and contact information submitted by wholesale trade buyers.

Information we collect

When you submit a quotation request or contact enquiry through this website, we collect:

  • Business name, contact person, business email address, and phone/WhatsApp number.
  • Store or warehouse delivery address.
  • Product selection, carton quantities, shipping preferences, and requested arrival dates.

How we use your information

We use your information strictly to:

  • Review your wholesale requirements and calculate freight, duties, clearance, and delivery terms.
  • Prepare and send your formal written delivered quotation.
  • Communicate with you regarding your enquiry, shipment arrangements, and order fulfillment.

Data security & sharing

We respect your privacy and do not sell, rent, or trade your contact information. Your details are shared only with vetted logistics and freight partners as necessary to arrange quotations and transport to your named delivery destination.

Contact us

If you have questions about your quotation or submitted information, contact us at inquiry@goshengiantgroup.com.
